# Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

**x-hands (推广之手)** does not collect, store, transmit or sell any data.

- It requests no browser permissions.
- Its content script runs only on x.com, twitter.com, mobile.x.com and mobile.twitter.com, and only draws an animation layer on top of the page.
- It does not read or change what you write, and makes no network requests (its images are bundled with the extension).

本插件不收集、不存储、不传输、不出售任何数据；不申请任何浏览器权限；只在 X 页面上叠加动画，不读取或修改你的帖子内容，也不发起任何网络请求。

Contact: https://github.com/yetone/x-hands/issues
